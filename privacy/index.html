Privacy Policy

Nabila01

Last updated: August 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Nabila01 ("the Application") is a desktop content automation application designed to help users manage and publish video content to TikTok.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Nabila01 and connect your TikTok account through TikTok's official authorization and API services.

2. Information We Access

When you authorize Nabila01 to connect with your TikTok account, the Application may access information permitted by the authorized TikTok API scopes.

This may include:

  • Basic TikTok profile information, such as your TikTok display name, avatar, and Open ID.
  • Information required to upload or publish video content to your TikTok account.
  • Information related to videos that can be accessed through the authorized video listing functionality.

3. TikTok Authorization

Nabila01 uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Access to your TikTok account is granted only after you explicitly authorize the Application through TikTok's authorization process.

Nabila01 does not attempt to bypass TikTok's authentication, authorization, or security mechanisms.

4. Local Processing

Nabila01 is designed to run on the user's local computer. Content prepared for publishing may therefore be processed locally on the user's device before being sent to TikTok through the authorized TikTok API.

Users are responsible for securing their own computer and protecting any locally stored application data.

5. Use of Information

Information accessed through TikTok is used only to provide the application's core functionality, including account connection, content uploading, content publishing, and authorized content management.

We do not sell TikTok user information to third parties.

6. Data Sharing

Nabila01 does not intentionally sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Information is transmitted to TikTok when necessary to perform actions that the user has explicitly authorized through the TikTok API.

7. Account Access and Revocation

Users may stop using Nabila01 and revoke the application's access to their TikTok account through the applicable TikTok account settings or authorization controls.

Once authorization is revoked, Nabila01 will no longer be able to use the revoked authorization to access the user's TikTok account.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to limit access to information used by the Application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Nabila01 is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Nabila01, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the application.
